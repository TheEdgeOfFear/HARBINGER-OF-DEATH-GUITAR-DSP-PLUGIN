# User Manual & How To Use — HARBINGER OF DEATH
### *By THE EDGE OF FEAR*

---

## 🎛️ Control Panel Reference

### 1. Main Drive & Gain Controls
- **GAIN**: Adjusts input preamp drive pushing into the octave generator and clipping waveshaper.
- **SQUARE / SHAPE**: Sets the severity and threshold of digital square-wave fuzz.
- **ASYMMETRY**: Introduces odd/even harmonic bias. Counter-clockwise produces symmetrical, smooth square-wave clipping; clockwise introduces raspy, gated asymmetry.
- **TONE**: Active tilt EQ balancing bass weight vs. treble cut.
- **LOW CUT**: High-pass filter eliminating unwanted sub-bass rumble below 80 Hz.
- **HI CUT**: Low-pass filter smoothing out fizzy top-end frequencies above 7 kHz.

---

### 2. Octave & Pitch Engine
- **OCTAVE MIX**: Controls the level of the generated upper octave overtone.
- **OCTAVE MODE Switch**:
  - +1 Octave: Classic screaming lead / octave fuzz.
  - +2 Octaves: Piercing high-frequency laser synth bite.
  - Dual (+1 & +2): Massive multi-octave wall of sound.
- **OCTAVE GLIDE / LAG**: Adjusts tracking response inertia.
  - *Low*: Instantaneous, tight tracking.
  - *High*: Trailing pitch-glide tails and dive-bomb glide artifacts.

---

### 3. Detune & Multi-Voice Engine
- **DETUNE**: Microtonal cents pitch-offset to widen riffs and solo leads.
- **VOICE MIX**: Controls the level of detuned secondary voices.

---

### 4. Chop & Rhythmic Stutter Modulator
- **CHOP RATE / SPEED**: Controls modulation speed:
  - In Free Mode: 0.1 Hz to 50 Hz.
  - In BPM Sync Mode: Tempo divisions (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, dotted & triplets).
- **BPM SYNC Switch**:
  - OFF: Manual speed knob control.
  - ON: Synchronizes the chopper directly to host DAW tempo.
- **STUTTER DEPTH**: Controls gating attenuation (from gentle tremolo to 100% hard silence gating).

---

### 5. Dual Footswitches & LINK Switch

- **CHOP Footswitch**: Engages the rhythmic stutter chopper.
- **TRASH Footswitch**: Engages high-gain saturation boost, extra resonance, and chaos sizzle.
- **LINK Slide Switch** *(Centered between footswitches)*:
  - **OFF**: CHOP and TRASH trigger independently.
  - **ON**: Pressing CHOP automatically triggers TRASH simultaneously, lighting up both glowing LED indicators.
- **PLUGIN ENABLE**: Master on/off bypass toggle.

---

## 🎹 MIDI Learn & Mapping Guide

Every parameter, knob, slider, and footswitch supports instant MIDI binding:

1. **Right-Click** any knob, toggle, or footswitch on the pedal interface.
2. Select **MIDI Learn** from the context menu.
3. Move any physical knob, expression pedal, or step on your MIDI footswitch. The parameter will immediately bind.
4. **Customizing Mappings**:
   - Click **MIDI Mappings** in the top bar to open the MIDI mapping table.
   - Adjust CC channel, CC number, minimum/maximum value scaling, or toggle polarity inversion.

## 💾 Presets System

HARBINGER OF DEATH includes a bank of pre-programmed factory presets accessible via the preset bar at the top:
- **Default Doom**: Heavy, crushing octave fuzz for standard and drop riffs.
- **Laser Octave Lead**: +2 octave screaming lead with trailing pitch-glide.
- **Machine Gun Chop**: BPM-synced 1/16th rhythmic stutter gate.
- **Trash Wall**: Dual octave + maximum trash gain boost.
- **Microtonal Widener**: Thick stereo detune chorus-fuzz for modern solos.
